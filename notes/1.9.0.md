- **Search the quiz with ⌘F.** Press ⌘F in Learning to open Kehikot's find bar. Every match in the questions and options on the page is highlighted and counted ("3 of 17"), and the page scrolls to the current one. Answers you have not earned yet are not on the page, so they are never found. The quiz editor keeps its own search.
- **No flash while it opens.** Kehikot's own loading cover stays up until the questions are drawn, then lifts on the finished page.
- **The container keeps its place.** How far down the questions you had scrolled is kept per container and comes back when you return to the kehikko.
- **A failing page says why.** If the page ever fails to draw, Kehikot shows the reason instead of a blank container.
