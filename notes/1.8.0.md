- **Agents can propose a change to the questions instead of writing it.** A proposal is drawn above the questions as a diff — what is there in red, what is proposed in green, the agent's reason beside it — with Accept, Decline, Decline with reason and Edit, then accept; a checkbox per change takes part of it. Nothing is written until you accept, and an accepted proposal goes into the undo trail like any other write.
- **The footer counts the proposals waiting**, with Previous and Next proposal beside the count, and a proposal says why it can no longer be applied when the file changed under it.

For agents: new tools `propose_change`, `list_proposals`, `read_proposal` and `withdraw_proposal`. Prefer `propose_change` over `reword_quiz` for a change the person should judge; `list_proposals` shows what became of each proposal, with the reason when it was declined — read it before proposing on that epic again.
